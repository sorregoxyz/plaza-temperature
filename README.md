# Daily Heat Register, Murcia 2026 

The artifact is built from a CSV dataset from the daily temperature register at Plaza Europa, recorded four times daily at 9:00, 13:00, 17:00, and 21:00 from May to September 2026. I took the daily temperature measurements using a handheld hygrometer. The register was made in two moments: first, I registered the ambient temperature holding the hygrometer in the air. Second, I located the hygrometer a few millimeters above the ground but without touching it. 

## Methodological considerations

The hygrometer provides precise temperature data. It constantly scans and displays the temperature of a space or material. When it is used, when the instrument is located near its target, there is a brief time for adjusting and providing a more stable temperature reading. In my experience, this takes about ten seconds. So each time I took a reading, I placed the hygrometer in position (in the air or near the ground) and waited 10 seconds to record a more stable temperature reading.
